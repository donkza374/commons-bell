Commons Bell — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Commons Bell ("the app") is a public-interest app that helps you see who represents you, how they vote, and what they say in the Chamber, using open data published by the Northern Ireland Assembly (and, in future, other UK legislatures).

Short version: the app does not create an account, does not require a login, and does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal data. There is no advertising and no analytics. Everything you do is kept on your device.

Data the app accesses

To show you live information, the app reads public, reusable data from the NI Assembly open-data service:

  • data.niassembly.gov.uk
  • aims.niassembly.gov.uk

This data is published under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

What the app stores (on your device only)

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone:

  • The area / constituency you selected (including any postcode you enter, resolved on-device against a bundled offline list).
  • The region / legislature you selected (NI Assembly, Westminster, Scottish Parliament, or Senedd).
  • Your own "cast votes" (Aye / No) on divisions, used only to show how your views compare with MLAs/MPs and to suggest a party that votes like you.
  • A cached copy of the latest data so the app opens quickly while offline.

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. The app has no backend server.

Personal data

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or any other personal identifier. Postcode lookups happen entirely on your device and are not transmitted anywhere. Android backups are disabled, so your data is not copied to cloud backup services.

Third-party services

The app uses no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only network requests it makes are to the public NI Assembly services listed above.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Clearing your data

You can delete everything the app stores at any time via Settings → Apps → Commons Bell → Clear data (or by uninstalling the app).

Children

The app is intended for users aged 16 and over. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published alongside a new app release.
